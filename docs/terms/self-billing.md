# Draft — for legal review

**Self-billing agreement — Talyvor Marketplace**
Version: draft-2026-10

This agreement is between **TALYVOR LTD**, 71-75 Shelton Street, Covent Garden, London, United Kingdom, WC2H 9JQ
("Talyvor", the customer), and **you, the seller** named in your seller tax details on the Talyvor Marketplace (the
supplier). You accept it when you save your seller tax details with this version as your self-billing agreement.

## What self-billing means

When a buyer pays for one of your listings, Talyvor sells it to the buyer and buys it from you. Instead of you sending
Talyvor an invoice for what you supply, Talyvor issues the invoice on your behalf: a **self-billed invoice**. Each weekly
payout statement is that invoice, for the earnings the payout pays.

## What Talyvor agrees to

1. To issue a self-billed invoice for every supply you make to Talyvor through the Marketplace, for as long as this
   agreement lasts, and to number each one from a series that is yours alone.
2. To show on each invoice your name, address and VAT registration number (if you have one), Talyvor's name, address
   and VAT registration number, the date, the net amount, the rate and amount of VAT, and any note that applies, such as
   the reverse charge.
3. To pay any VAT the invoice shows together with the payout it belongs to.
4. To tell you if Talyvor's VAT registration number changes or Talyvor stops being registered for VAT.

## What you agree to

1. To accept the self-billed invoices Talyvor issues for your supplies, and not to issue your own sales invoices for
   them.
2. To tell Talyvor at once, by updating your seller tax details, if you register for VAT, deregister, or your VAT
   registration number changes, and if you sell or transfer all or part of your business.
3. That the VAT on each invoice is worked out from the tax details you give, and that you keep those details accurate.
4. To account for the VAT shown on each invoice to your tax authority, where you are registered for VAT.

## Taxes

A VAT-registered seller in the United Kingdom has UK VAT added to their payout at the rate in force. A business seller
outside the United Kingdom is not charged VAT: the invoice carries the reverse-charge note, and Talyvor accounts for
the VAT. A seller who is not registered for VAT is not charged any. Until Talyvor's accountant confirms this treatment,
every self-billed invoice is issued at zero VAT and reads "VAT on your supply: under review".

## How long it lasts

This agreement starts on the day you accept it and lasts for twelve months. It renews for another twelve months each
time unless either of us ends it, which either of us may do at any time by notice. Ending it does not affect invoices
already issued.

## Law

This agreement is governed by the law of England and Wales.
