## The Talyvor remix licence

This licence applies when you press **Remix** on a marketplace listing whose owner allows remixes, and you accept
it. It covers the one version of the listing you open.

1. **What you may do.** You may open the listing version's prompt, skill, agent, evaluation or pipeline, change it,
   and publish what you make on the Talyvor marketplace as your own listing.
2. **Credit and the family tree.** A listing you publish from it names the version you opened as its parent. That
   link is permanent and is shown to anyone who reads your listing's family tree.
3. **Royalty.** If the listing asks a royalty, the share it asked when you accepted this licence is locked for the
   version you opened. That share of each sale of your remix is paid to the original's owner through the
   marketplace. A later change to the owner's terms does not change the share you accepted.
4. **Free remixes.** If the listing is free to remix, no share of your remix's sales is owed to its owner.
5. **Where you may use it.** The licence covers building on the listing within the Talyvor marketplace. It does not
   let you copy the original out of Talyvor, resell it unchanged, or remove its owner's credit.
6. **Your own work.** What you add is yours. You are responsible for it in the same way as for any listing you
   publish, and it is reviewed in the same way.
7. **Withdrawal.** An owner who stops allowing remixes does not take back a licence already accepted: remixes made
   under it, and remixes you publish later from the version you opened, keep the share you accepted.
